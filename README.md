## About VEYRO

**VEYRO** is a modern productivity application designed to help you take control of your everyday life.

From setting monthly goals to organising personal notes and tracking habits, VEYRO brings essential productivity tools together in one streamlined desktop experience.

Designed with simplicity, functionality and aesthetics in mind, VEYRO helps you focus on what truly matters.

---

## Features

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| 🎯 Goals Tracker | Create, organise and monitor your goals. |

| 📊 Habit Tracker | Build consistency and monitor your habits. |
